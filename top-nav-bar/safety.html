Stay Safe, Confident, and Prepared on Welsh Roads

Safety is at the heart of everything we do at Pedal Cymru. Whether you are riding along dedicated traffic-free cycle paths or navigating busy town roads, following essential safety rules ensures a secure and confident journey for yourself and other road users.

The Essential ABC Pre-Ride Check

Before setting off on any bike ride, always perform the quick 30-second ABC Check to ensure your bicycle is in safe working condition:

  1. A is for Air: Check your tyre pressure by squeezing the sides. Ensure tyres are firm and free of embedded glass or thorns.
  2. B is for Brakes: Squeeze your front and rear brake levers firmly. Ensure the brake pads grip the rim or disc firmly and the bike cannot roll forward.
  3. C is for Chain and Crank: Ensure your chain is clean, oiled, and running smoothly. Spin the pedals backwards to check for stiff links or grinding gear cables.

Road Position and Highway Code Tips

Ride at least 0.75 metres away from the curb to avoid drain covers and road debris. Always use clear hand signals well in advance when turning, and maintain eye contact with drivers at junctions.

Quick Safety Checklist

  • Helmet Fit: Wear a correctly fitted helmet positioned level on your head, sitting two fingers above your eyebrows.
  • High-Visibility: Wear bright or fluorescent clothing by day, and reflective gear in low light.
  • Bike Lights: Use a white front light and a red rear light at dusk, at night, or in poor weather.
  • Passing Distance: Motorists should give cyclists at least 1.5 metres of space when overtaking.
